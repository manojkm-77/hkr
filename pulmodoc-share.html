Core specialty topics

A broad map of the clinical subjects represented in the library.

01 · Infections

Approach common and complex respiratory infections, from community-acquired pneumonia to tuberculosis and fungal disease.

  • CAP, HAP and ventilator-associated pneumonia
  • Drug-sensitive and drug-resistant TB
  • Fungal disease, ABPA and aspergilloma

02 · Airway diseases

Review obstructive airway disease mechanisms, phenotypes, assessment and long-term treatment.

  • Asthma phenotypes and severe asthma
  • COPD, exacerbations and prevention
  • Bronchiectasis and cystic fibrosis

03 · Interstitial lung disease

Connect clinical presentation, pulmonary function, imaging patterns and multidisciplinary diagnosis.

  • IPF and usual interstitial pneumonia
  • Connective-tissue disease associated ILD
  • Hypersensitivity pneumonitis and sarcoidosis

04 · Pulmonary vascular disease

Build a structured approach to vascular causes of dyspnea and right-heart strain.

  • Pulmonary embolism and risk assessment
  • Pulmonary hypertension groups
  • Right ventricular assessment and follow-up

05 · Pleural & mediastinal disease

Review pleural fluid, air, infection and mass evaluation alongside practical procedures.

  • Pleural effusion and Light’s criteria
  • Pneumothorax, empyema and chest drains
  • Mediastinal lesions and diagnostic pathways

06 · Thoracic oncology

Summarize lung cancer evaluation, staging concepts and treatment pathways.

  • Histology and diagnostic sampling
  • TNM staging principles
  • Targeted therapy and immunotherapy overview

07 · Sleep & ventilation

Cover sleep-disordered breathing and the respiratory support strategies used in acute care.

  • Obstructive sleep apnea assessment
  • Noninvasive ventilation and troubleshooting
  • ARDS and lung-protective ventilation

08 · Critical care

Organize assessment of respiratory failure, monitoring and escalation in the intensive care setting.

  • Oxygen delivery and respiratory support
  • Mechanical ventilation fundamentals
  • Sepsis, shock and critical illness review

09 · Procedures & diagnostics

Understand indications, preparation, interpretation and safety considerations for common investigations.

  • Spirometry, lung volumes and DLCO
  • Bronchoscopy, BAL and biopsy
  • Thoracentesis and pleural biopsy

Study and clinical tools

Examples of practical workflows described across the suite.

ABG interpretation

Work through pH, primary disorder, expected compensation, anion gap and oxygenation. Review concepts such as Winter’s formula and delta ratio as learning aids.

Severity scores

Revise commonly used scoring frameworks, including CURB-65 for pneumonia and Wells criteria for pulmonary embolism, alongside their intended context.

Imaging practice

Use a consistent approach to chest radiographs and thoracic CT: image quality, anatomy, pattern recognition, differential diagnosis and clinical correlation.

Exam preparation

Move between question practice, image spotters, bedside examination outlines, long-case structure and practical viva prompts.

Emergency revision

Review the themes in acute asthma, COPD exacerbation, severe pneumonia, pulmonary embolism, hemoptysis and acute respiratory distress syndrome.

Device technique

Reinforce inhaler education topics for pressurized metered-dose inhalers, spacers and dry-powder devices, including patient demonstration and teach-back.
