🚨 Acute Severe / Near-Fatal Asthma RICU Resuscitation Bundle

Immediate 0–60 Minute Multi-Modal Bronchodilator & Anti-Inflammatory Protocol

EMERGENCY ADMISSION ORDER SHEET — ACUTE SEVERE / NEAR-FATAL ASTHMA
DIAGNOSIS: Acute Severe Asthma (Silent chest / PEFR < 33% / PaCO2 ≥ 42 mmHg)
LOCATION: Respiratory Intensive Care Unit (RICU) / High Dependency Unit

1. POSITIONING & MONITORING:
   - Propped-up sitting position (45–90 degrees). Do not force patient supine.
   - Continuous multi-parameter monitoring: SpO2, ECG lead II, NIBP every 15 min.
   - Target SpO2: 93–95% (Avoid hyperoxia-induced V/Q mismatch).

2. HIGH-FLOW CONTROLLED OXYGEN:
   - Oxygen via Venturi mask or tight nasal prongs titrated to SpO2 93–95%.

3. CONTINUOUS / STACKED INHALED BRONCHODILATION:
   - Nebulized Salbutamol 5.0 mg + Ipratropium Bromide 0.5 mg in 3 mL NS.
   - Repeat back-to-back every 20 minutes for first 3 doses (0, 20, 40 min).
   - Driven by oxygen at 6–8 L/min (never air-driven in severe hypoxemia).

4. SYSTEMIC CORTICOSTEROIDS (IMMEDIATE):
   - Inj. Hydrocortisone 200 mg IV stat, then 100 mg IV Q6H
     (OR Inj. Methylprednisolone 40–80 mg IV stat, then 40 mg IV Q12H).

5. INTRAVENOUS MAGNESIUM SULFATE:
   - Inj. Magnesium Sulfate (MgSO4) 2.0 g (50% solution, 4 mL) diluted in 100 mL 5% Dextrose
     infused IV over 20 minutes with continuous cardiac monitoring.

6. FLUIDS & ELECTROLYTES:
   - IV Normal Saline (0.9% NaCl) at 75–100 mL/hr (Correct insensible dehydration from tachypnea).
   - Monitor Serum Potassium (High-dose salbutamol drives K+ intracellularly; supplement if K < 3.8).

7. ICU / INTUBATION READINESS:
   - PaCO2 > 42 mmHg with worsening exhaustion or altered mental status is an absolute alert.
   - Ketamine 1–2 mg/kg IV preferred for RSI (bronchodilating NMDA antagonist).
   - Avoid paralytic-steroid prolonged myopathy.

💨 AECOPD Acute Respiratory Acidosis & NIV (BiPAP) Protocol

GOLD 2024–2026 Non-Invasive Positive Pressure Ventilation Bundle

EMERGENCY ADMISSION ORDER SHEET — AECOPD WITH ACUTE RESPIRATORY ACIDOSIS
DIAGNOSIS: Acute Exacerbation of COPD (GOLD Group E) with Type 2 Respiratory Failure (pH < 7.35, PaCO2 > 45 mmHg)
LOCATION: RICU / Respiratory Stepdown

1. CONTROLLED OXYGEN TARGET:
   - Oxygen titrated strictly via 24%–28% Venturi mask to SpO2 88–92%.
   - (AVOID excessive FiO2: Prevents Haldane effect and abolition of hypoxic vasoconstriction).

2. NON-INVASIVE VENTILATION (NIV / BiPAP) PROTOCOL:
   - Mode: Spontaneous/Timed (S/T) with well-fitted oronasal mask.
   - Initial IPAP (Inspiratory Pressure): 10–12 cmH2O.
   - Initial EPAP (Expiratory Pressure): 4–5 cmH2O (Counteracts intrinsic PEEP / auto-PEEP).
   - Titration: Increase IPAP by 2 cmH2O every 15–30 min based on tidal volume (Target 6–8 mL/kg) and tolerability up to 16–20 cmH2O.
   - Backup Rate: 12–14 breaths/min; Rise time: 0.1–0.2 sec.
   - MANDATORY: Repeat ABG at 1 HOUR post-NIV initiation (Look for improvement in pH > 7.35 and PaCO2 reduction).

3. BRONCHODILATION:
   - Nebulized Levosalbutamol 1.25 mg + Ipratropium Bromide 0.5 mg Q4H via NIV nebulizer T-piece.

4. SYSTEMIC CORTICOSTEROIDS:
   - Tab / Inj. Prednisolone 40 mg PO/IV once daily for 5 days strictly (REDUCE trial protocol).

5. ANTIMICROBIAL THERAPY (Anthonisen Type 1 Criteria):
   - Inj. Amoxicillin-Clavulanate 1.2 g IV Q8H (or Inj. Cefotaxime 1 g IV Q8H) for 5–7 days.
   - Azithromycin 500 mg PO on Day 1, then 250 mg daily for 4 days if atypical coverage needed.

🩸 Massive Hemoptysis Immediate Resuscitation SOP

>100 mL/hr or >400 mL/24 hr Airway Protection & Bronchial Artery Embolization Bundle

EMERGENCY RESUSCITATION ORDER SHEET — MASSIVE HEMOPTYSIS
DIAGNOSIS: Massive Hemoptysis (>200 mL acute or >600 mL/24 hr)
PRIORITY: AIRWAY PROTECTION (Death is from ASPHYXIATION, not hypovolemia)

1. POSITIONING:
   - Place patient in LATERAL DECUBITUS position with the BLEEDING LUNG DOWN.
   - (Prevents blood from pooling into the contralateral healthy lung).

2. RESUSCITATION & VASCULAR ACCESS:
   - Secure TWO large-bore IV cannulae (16G or 14G).
   - Draw blood immediately for: Blood Grouping & Crossmatch (4 units PRBCs), CBC, PT/INR, aPTT, Platelets, Renal Function.
   - Normal Saline IV infusion to maintain MAP ≥ 65 mmHg.

3. PHARMACOTHERAPY:
   - Inj. Tranexamic Acid (TXA) 1.0 g IV slow infusion over 10 min stat, then 1.0 g IV Q8H
     (Nebulized TXA: 500 mg in 5 mL NS via nebulizer can be co-administered for topical hemostasis).
   - Inj. Terlipressin 1.0 mg IV stat (or Vasopressin 0.2 units/min) with cardiac caution.
   - Cough suppression: Syrup Codeine Phosphate 10–15 mL PO Q6H (prevent violent paroxysms).

4. DEFINITIVE RADIOLOGY & PULMONOLOGY ALERT:
   - Urgent contrast-enhanced CT of Thorax with Angiography (CTPA + Bronchial Artery Angio protocol).
   - Alert Interventional Radiology for emergency Bronchial Artery Embolization (BAE).
   - Prepare Flexible Bronchoscope with cold saline lavage (4 deg C) and topical 1:20,000 adrenaline.

🛠️ Local Launcher Operations & Batch Diagnostics

The backend runs on Python's built-in standard library with zero third-party pip dependencies.

1. How launch_app.bat Works

When you double-click launch_app.bat, it sets the directory to %~dp0 (this folder), checks for Python 3 in your PATH, and runs python pulmo_server.py. The server binds to port 8000 (or finds the next open port if 8000 is occupied) and automatically opens your default web browser.

2. Starting from PowerShell / Terminal

You can also start the server directly by running:

cd "C:\Users\harshith k r\Desktop\textbooks"; python pulmo_server.py

3. Port Already in Use (Address already in use)

pulmo_server.py has an intelligent port finder that automatically probes ports 8000 through 8010. If another process has port 8000 occupied, it will smoothly start on 8001 without crashing.
